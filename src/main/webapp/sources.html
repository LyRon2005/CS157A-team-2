Data sources & coverage

The map currently shows Main Campus only: 57 footprints. The 94 footprints listed below remain in the source archive. The overview faces 58.8° from the Boccardo Gate side toward Lucas Gate. Confirming a building selection zooms to it. SJSU's Facilities Development Plan, page 21 identifies Boccardo Gateway at Paseo de San Carlos and 4th Street. SJSU has several entrances; Boccardo is the reference for this sjsuclassrooms.

The 2D ground layer uses the OSM snapshot: 490 path segments, 147 road segments, 82 green areas, 2 water areas and 10 tree locations within Main Campus. Counts represent clipped map features, not distinct streets. Tree canopies use a symbolic 2.2 m radius, not a measured size. The camera provides an elevated overview from the entrance side, not an eye-level view.

Indoor floor directory

The old schematic floor bands and estimated floor buttons have been removed. The indoor browser uses published university plans with source dates. Historical room lists are reference material, not current availability. Unknown floors, room locations and main-lobby photos are left unverified, rather than filled with invented data. See the indoor sources below.

This model uses public mapping data. It is not a certified survey of all SJSU property. Footprints use geographic coordinates, but the sources may be outdated or incomplete. Current heights and complete coverage have not been verified.

  • Official SJSU map: names, locations and outdoor building/facility listings. Indoor rooms, non-SJSU ParkSJ garages and commercial locations are excluded.
  • OpenStreetMap — © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL: footprints from the map API, preserving courtyard holes and multipolygon relations. Area and bounding dimensions are calculated in UTM zone 10N (EPSG:32610). Length and width describe the minimum rotated rectangle enclosing a structure, not individual wall dimensions.
  • City of San José Building Footprints: supplemental heights where footprint overlap exceeds an IoU of 0.72, converted from US survey feet to meters. The metadata describes a 2006 LiDAR survey with no subsequent updates. These heights are not assigned to identified newer buildings: ISB, SRAC, CV2, Habbas, Student Wellness and South Campus Garage.
  • unknown heights are displayed as 0.5 m footprint plates, not estimated building heights. OSM floor counts are stored separately and are not converted into heights.
  • Duplicate names and connected structures are recorded as aliases. Unnamed ancillary structures retain their OSM identifiers. One facility may contain multiple footprints; footprint counts are not administrative building counts.
  • The source archive includes structures matched to the SJSU directory, within the OSM campus boundary or attributed to SJSU in OSM. The current map filters this archive to the Main Campus boundary.
  • Legacy sample room labels in the archived dataset are not live room inventory. The old TSV booking API has been removed.
