# Third-party data and software

- MapLibre GL JS 5.6.2 is bundled under `src/main/webapp/vendor/` with its BSD 3-Clause license in `LICENSE-MapLibre.txt`. Downloaded from the published npm package at https://unpkg.com/maplibre-gl@5.6.2/ .
- OpenStreetMap data: © OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under ODbL. Attribution and license: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . Geographic data exports are included for inspection. Original source snapshots and rebuild tools are preserved in Git at checkpoint/before-cleanup-2026-10-19. The map preserves attribution.
- City of San José GIS: https://geo.sanjoseca.gov/server/rest/services/DOT/DOT_Basemap_InfrastructureMaintenance/MapServer/13 . Historical metadata and limitations are described on the Sources & coverage page.
- Official SJSU directory and map: https://map.sjsu.edu/ and https://www.sjsu.edu/printmaps/ . Used for public building names, locations and source references.

This student prototype is not an official SJSU booking service. No affiliation or endorsement is implied. It does not reproduce the proprietary Concept3D map client or 3D assets.

- Indoor floor-plan JPEGs in src/main/webapp/images/floors are rendered from the SJSU Campus Utilization Report (historical Fall 2011 room roster) and the Fall 2024 Engineering Buildings guide. Each floor includes its university source URL and date warning in building-directory.js. Source drawings retain their original labels. URLs: https://www.sjsu.edu/fdo/docs/SJSU_Campus_Utilization_Report.pdf and https://www.sjsu.edu/engineering/docs/FA24%20Engineering%20Buildings.pdf .
- Engineering upper-level lobby and Clark lobby reference photos are extracted from page 66 of the SJSU Facility Development Plan 2017: https://www.sjsu.edu/campusmasterplan/docs/SJSU_Facility_Development_Plan_2017.pdf . They are historical university reference material; they are not verified main-entrance photos. Copyright remains with the original owners. No open-content license is asserted for these university drawings/photos.
- ISB approximate rendering height uses the 148 ft planning envelope, not a measured as-built height: https://www.sjsu.edu/fdo/docs/ISB_Final%20MND.pdf (page 14).

- Expanded building imagery: Wikimedia Commons photographs retain individual author, license and source URLs in building-photos.js. SJSU photos from Housing, Student Union, Wellness, School of Management and Nursing pages retain links to the originating pages. Health Building photograph: Maya Carlyle, November 2021. University photos are attributed reference material; no open license is asserted. South and West Garage images are official location diagrams from https://www.sjsu.edu/parking/maps/garages.php , not exterior photographs. Missing-photo outlines are derived from the existing attributed OpenStreetMap footprints and explicitly labeled.
