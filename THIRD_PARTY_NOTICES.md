# Third-party data and software

- MapLibre GL JS 5.6.2 is bundled under `web/vendor/` with its BSD 3-Clause license in `LICENSE-MapLibre.txt`. Downloaded from the published npm package at https://unpkg.com/maplibre-gl@5.6.2/ .
- OpenStreetMap data: © OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under ODbL. Attribution and license: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . Geographic data exports and source snapshots are included for inspection and rebuilding. The map preserves attribution.
- City of San José GIS: https://geo.sanjoseca.gov/server/rest/services/DOT/DOT_Basemap_InfrastructureMaintenance/MapServer/13 . Historical metadata and limitations are described on the Sources & coverage page.
- Official SJSU directory and map: https://map.sjsu.edu/ and https://www.sjsu.edu/printmaps/ . Used for public building names, locations and source references.
