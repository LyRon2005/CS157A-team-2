# Main-campus floor research

Reviewed September 22, 2026. Covers all 57 footprints currently rendered, including 12 unnamed ancillary structures; this is not a claim of complete university property coverage.

30 buildings have a sourced numerical count: 21 use the March 13, 2015 assessment reproduced in Appendix E of the 2017 Facilities Development Plan, and 9 use separate university or architect sources. Historical counts are explicitly labeled in the homepage. Ten additional footprints have partial, conflicting, or special-level evidence. Seventeen remain unresolved.

## Limits

A published count does not establish measured floor elevations, room layouts, current bookable levels, or uniform height across wings. Floor buttons require explicitly documented level labels. No equal-height bands are generated. No counts are inferred from height or OSM tags. The assessment’s Joe West count (9) conflicts with later housing documents (12); the existing 12-floor record is retained. Possible name matches for Data Center / Computer Center and corporation-yard facilities are not treated as verified matches.
